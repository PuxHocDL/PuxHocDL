## About Me

I am an **AI Researcher & Engineer** at Rosenxt Group and a researcher at the University of Information Technology (UIT), VNU-HCM.

My work focuses on turning research ideas into practical, reliable AI systems. I work across Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Multi-Agent Systems, and Reinforcement Learning.

My research spans opinion mining, aspect-based sentiment analysis, coreference resolution, cross-lingual NLP, and gradient-free optimization of neural architectures. Alongside academic research, I build and deploy production-grade AI systems, from multilingual privacy-preserving NLP tools to RAG applications and data agents.

**Research & Engineering:** Turning research into AI systems that ship.

## Selected Publications

### 2026

**Enhancing Cross-Lingual Target Aspect Sentiment Detection via Instruction-Tuned Small Language Models**  
*Neural Computing and Applications (Q1 Journal) · First Author*

- Cross-lingual TASD benchmark of parameter-efficient fine-tuned instruction models across eight languages.
- Investigates structured extraction prompting for cross-lingual transfer.

**EduPulse: A Practical LLM-Enhanced Opinion Mining System for Vietnamese Student Feedback in Educational Platforms**  
*EACL 2026 Industry Track · First Author*

- Hybrid LLM and lightweight model pipeline for Vietnamese student feedback analysis.
- Achieves 92% accuracy, improving 7.6% over traditional approaches.

**Improving Vietnamese Text Classification with Multi-Head Attention Pooling in Pre-trained Language Models**  
*MAPR 2026 · Co-Author*

- Introduces Multi-Head Attention Pooling for pretrained language models.
- Improves MRPC F1 by 3.00 points using PhoBERT-base.

**SAM-Anchored DINOv3 Soft Calibration for Weakly Supervised Semantic Segmentation**  
*MAPR 2026 · Co-Author*

- Explores SAM-anchored soft calibration for weakly supervised semantic segmentation.

### 2025

**PuxAI at QIAS 2025: Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Islamic Inheritance and Knowledge Reasoning**  
*ArabicNLP 2025 @ EMNLP · First Author*

- Multi-agent RAG system for Islamic knowledge and inheritance reasoning.
- 1st Place in Knowledge Assessment and 2nd Place in Inheritance Reasoning.

**Few-Shot Coreference Resolution with Semantic Difficulty Metrics and In-Context Learning**  
*CODI-CRAC 2025 @ EMNLP · First Author*

- Few-shot coreference resolution using semantic difficulty metrics and LLM-based in-context learning.
- 3rd Place in the LLM Track.

**Optimization of Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks for Reinforcement Learning via NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies**  
*SOICT 2025 · Co-Author*

- Gradient-free evolutionary optimization of KAN architectures and node-level functions.

**PuxAI at MentalRiskES 2025: RoBERTuito with Bidirectional LSTM for Early Gambling Disorder Detection**  
*IberLEF 2025 Working Notes · First Author*

- Combines RoBERTuito and BiLSTM for early gambling disorder detection.
- Ranked 21st in Task 1 and 10th in Task 2.
